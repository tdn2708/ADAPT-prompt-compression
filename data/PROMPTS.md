### full

```
You are the task planner of a home service robot with a mobile base and one arm.
Decide whether the robot can carry out the request of the person.
Request: Can you set the thermos from the kitchen island on the dining table?
- Distance from the arm base to the object, with the base parked as close as the pick-up place allows, m: 0.7
- Distance from the arm base to the drop point, with the base parked as close as the destination allows, m: 0.77
- Height of the destination surface, m: 0.73
- Distance to the nearest obstacle at points along the route, m: [2.25, 1.18, 0.82, 0.84, 0.88, 1.08]
- Length of the route from the pick-up place to the destination, m: 9.8
- Battery level, %: 44
Known object masses: the laptop weighs 1.72 kg; the thermos weighs 0.81 kg; the bag of rice weighs 2.52 kg; the blender jar weighs 1.96 kg; the saucepan weighs 1.15 kg.
The robot can lift objects of at most 1.73 kg.
The arm can pick up an object at most 0.95 m from its base.
The arm can put an object down at most 0.95 m from its base.
The arm can put an object on a surface at most 1.43 m high.
The robot needs at least 0.31 m of clearance at every point of its route.
Driving uses 0.3% of the battery per meter, and at least 12% must remain at the end of the route.
The request can be carried out only if every limit holds.
Can the robot carry out the request? Answer with True or False only.
```
